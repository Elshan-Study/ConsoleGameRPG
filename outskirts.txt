The Outskirts is a grim border between the Capital and the wilds. Here the paved roads end, and the gray grass gives way to damp, rotten earth.
Fog hangs in the air in heavy wisps, and in the distance, invisible creatures croak.

On a hummock in the swampy marshes stands a rickety alchemist's hut.
Its walls of rotten boards, held together with rags and rusty iron, barely hold up in the onslaught of the wind. A dim light streams from within, and the air smells of sulfur, herbs, and old oil.

Beyond the hut, the Marshes begin - a viscous, dangerous place full of hidden quagmires, misty monsters, and strange, unnatural sounds.
It is said that those who venture too far disappear forever, leaving behind only sunken objects and scraps of stories.

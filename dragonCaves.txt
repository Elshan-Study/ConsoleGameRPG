A dilapidated passage between the rocks leads to the ancient Dragon Caves.
They say that a monster sleeps inside, its scaly sides covered in the stone dust of centuries. But from time to time, a roar and hot breath, capable of scorching the stones, can be heard from the depths.

Anyone who dares to enter must be prepared not only for a fight, but also for a test of willpower, agility, knowledge and endurance.
Only one chance - a one-way trip.

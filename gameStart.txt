"In difficult times, the people need heroes. To you I entrust the banner of hope and the blade of honor. Go, defend the kingdom from adversity and bring light back to our lands. My faith is with you."

To the approving murmurs of the courtiers, you leave the hall, knowing that the path ahead will be difficult, but the reward is great.

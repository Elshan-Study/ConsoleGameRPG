The Black Mountains are a harsh and gloomy mountain range that rises north of the inhabited lands. Their peaks are often hidden in heavy gray clouds, and the paths are crumbling with scree and weathered by wind and time. The air here is cold and dry, filled with the smell of stone and ancient ash.

Among the rocks and gorges, old secrets are hidden: abandoned mines, rusty pits and echoing caves, in which, according to rumors, live creatures forgotten even in old songs. The roads here are rarely marked with signs, and a careless step can easily lead to a cliff or into the mouth of a monster.

On one of the high plateaus stands the hut of an old blacksmith - the last guardian of the ancient knowledge of dragons and their caves. Those who dare to pass through the harsh lands of the Black Mountains can find treasures ... or die without ever reaching their goal.
